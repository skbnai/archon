An AI-First enterprise (Level 5) treats AI as the primary tool for solving business problems. An AI-Native enterprise (Level 6) is qualitatively different: AI is not a tool the enterprise uses. AI is the operating fabric of the enterprise. The distinction maps to how the internet transformed companies — AI-First is like having a website; AI-Native is like being Amazon.

## Beyond AI-First: The AI-Native Tier (Level 6)

### Six Defining Characteristics

1. **Constitutionally governed agents:** Every agent operates under a ratified AI constitution enforced at runtime. Governance is not an overlay — it is the foundation. Agents cannot operate without constitutional compliance.

2. **Sovereign AI infrastructure:** The enterprise controls its AI stack: sovereign compute, sovereign model weights, sovereign data, sovereign identity. No foreign entity holds a kill switch over mission-critical AI capability.

3. **Continuous alignment:** AI systems continuously self-monitor for goal drift, reward hacking, and constitutional violation. Alignment is not a one-time training process but an ongoing runtime guarantee.

4. **Democratic oversight:** Affected stakeholders (employees, customers, communities) have meaningful input into AI constitutional principles. Collective Constitutional AI processes used for high-impact systems. Public AI register maintained.

5. **Self-healing AI systems:** AI systems detect degradation, trigger retraining, renegotiate resources, restore safe operating state — without human intervention for standard scenarios. Humans govern the policies within which self-healing operates.

6. **AI-native culture:** The workforce thinks in AI-native ways: agents as colleagues, data as infrastructure, governance as engineering. AI literacy is universal; AI fluency is a professional baseline.

## The Eight-Dimension AI Maturity Assessment

Score each dimension 0–5. Maximum total = 40. Your total maps to maturity Level 1–6.

### Dimension 1: AI Strategy & Executive Sponsorship

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 0 | No formal AI strategy; AI initiatives are ad hoc |
| 1 | AI vision exists at executive level but not documented |
| 2 | AI strategy documented; investment roadmap defined |
| 3 | AI strategy integrated into enterprise strategy; board-level commitment |
| 4 | AI strategy drives enterprise operating model; CAIO role with board access |
| 5 | Enterprise mission cannot be separated from AI; board-level constitutional AI commitment; sovereign AI policy |

### Dimension 2: Data Infrastructure & Sovereignty

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Data is siloed; no AI-ready data infrastructure |
| 1 | Basic data lake exists; minimal AI feature engineering |
| 2 | Unified data platform; data governance in place |
| 3 | AI-ready feature stores; data lineage; sovereignty controls for regulated data |
| 4 | Real-time AI data pipelines; full data lineage; sovereign data infrastructure for Tier 1 |
| 5 | Sovereign data fabric; AI curates its own knowledge; zero foreign dependency for critical data |

### Dimension 3: AI Platform & MLOps Maturity

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|

| 3 | Eval gate framework; drift detection; cost monitoring; multi-env deployment |
| 4 | Sovereign AI landing zone; constitutional policy engine integrated; full AIOps |
| 5 | Sovereign AI OS; self-healing model pipelines; constitutional enforcement at platform level |
