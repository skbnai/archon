## How to Use This Report in a Consulting Engagement

### Phase 1 — Discovery (Week 1–2)

- Use Part 17 — Maturity Assessment to score the client
- Use Part 2 — Operating Models to identify current vs. target model
- Use Part 18 — Case Studies for benchmarking

### Phase 2 — Design (Week 2–4)

- Use Part 6 — Governance for governance design
- Use Part 8 — Roles for org design
- Use Part 7 + 10 for platform design

### Phase 3 — Roadmap (Week 4–6)

- Use Part 17 — Roadmap for phased roadmap
- Use Part 16 — Financial Model for business case
- Use Part 19 — Deliverables (this page) to identify artifacts to produce

### Phase 4 — Delivery

- Use Part 3–5 for delivery lifecycle design
- Use Part 9 — Processes for operational process design
- Use Part 11 — DevSecOps for engineering process design
