# Industry Deep Dives: Banking, Healthcare & Retail

Why this matters: AI transformation looks different in every industry. Understanding industry-specific dynamics, regulations, and competitive threats is essential for strategy.

## Banking & Financial Services

### Competitive Dynamics

Banking faces structural disruption driven by:
- Neobanks/challenger banks (Monzo, N26, Revolut) with zero-fee propositions
- Embedded finance—financial services inside non-financial platforms
- Open Banking regulations mandating data portability (PSD2, CDR)
- Foundation model AI compressing cost of financial intelligence

### Top 5 AI Opportunities

1. **Real-time fraud prevention** — ML models at sub-100ms latency; $2-5B annual fraud loss prevention at major banks
2. **AI-powered credit decisioning** — Alternative data expanding credit access; faster decisions
3. **Intelligent document processing** — KYC extraction, loan processing; 60-80% cost reduction
4. **GenAI-powered research and advisory** — AI equity research, financial planning copilot
5. **Predictive churn prevention** — Identify customers at risk 30-90 days before; intervention

### Transformation Roadmap Pattern

**Year 1: Foundation** → Core data modernization, cloud migration, AI platform build, fraud detection & KYC AI

**Year 2-3: Scale** → AI-powered credit underwriting, customer service transformation, regulatory AI, core banking modernization

**Year 4-5: Differentiate** → GenAI-powered advisory at scale, AI-native products, agentic AI in treasury

## Healthcare

### Competitive Dynamics

Healthcare is fragmented, heavily regulated, facing:
- Information asymmetry between patients, providers, payers
- Misaligned incentives (fee-for-service creates volume incentives, not outcome incentives)
- Interoperability gaps (EHR systems don't communicate)
- Regulatory conservatism (FDA approval, liability concerns)

### Top 5 AI Opportunities

1. **Radiology AI** — Automated cancer screening; 20-40% detection rate improvement; FDA-approved products exist
2. **Prior authorization automation** — $35B administrative cost in US payer-provider friction; AI reduces days to minutes
3. **Clinical documentation (ambient AI)** — AI auto-generates clinical notes; 2-3 hours saved per physician/day
4. **Predictive readmission prevention** — Identify high-risk patients; enable interventions
5. **Drug discovery acceleration** — AI-driven target identification, molecular design; 20-40% timeline reduction

### Transformation Roadmap Pattern

**Year 1: Administrative AI** → Coding/billing AI, claims processing, scheduling

**Year 2-3: Clinical AI** → Diagnostic support, clinical documentation, population health

**Year 4-5: AI-Integrated Care** → AI-augmented clinical pathways, virtual care with AI triage

## Retail & Consumer

### Competitive Dynamics

Retail reshaped by:
- E-commerce channel shift
- Marketplace aggregation (Amazon, Alibaba)
- Direct-to-consumer brand strategies
- Convergence of physical and digital retail (unified commerce)

### Top 5 AI Opportunities

1. **Hyper-personalisation** — AI-powered 1:1 recommendations; 15-30% conversion uplift documented at scale
2. **Intelligent demand forecasting** — AI incorporating weather, social trends, competitor promotions; 20-40% overstock reduction, 10-15% stockout reduction
3. **Dynamic pricing** — Real-time optimization; 2-5% gross margin improvement
4. **AI-powered search** — Semantic and visual search; significant conversion uplift
5. **Autonomous replenishment** — ML-driven replenishment signals; 15-25% inventory carrying cost reduction

### Transformation Roadmap Pattern

**Year 1: Data foundation + Quick-win AI** → Unified commerce data layer, recommendation engine, email personalization

**Year 2-3: Operational AI** → Demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, AI customer service

**Year 4-5: AI-Native Retail** → Autonomous store models, AI buyer, agentic supply chain
