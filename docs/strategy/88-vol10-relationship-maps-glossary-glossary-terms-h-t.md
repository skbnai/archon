# Enterprise Glossary: H-T

Continuing the enterprise glossary (H through T).

## H

**Hallucination**: AI system generating content that appears credible but is factually incorrect (AI domain).

**HITL (Human-in-the-Loop)**: Design pattern requiring human review and approval before AI action proceeds (AI domain).

**Horizon Model (McKinsey)**: Three-horizon framework for managing core (H1), emerging (H2), and exploratory (H3) businesses (Strategy domain).

**Hoshin Kanri**: Japanese strategic planning methodology aligning the entire organization to 3–5 priorities (Strategy domain).

**HQ (Headquarters)**: Central function of a corporation setting strategy and governance for business units (Organization domain).

## I–J

**IAM (Identity and Access Management)**: Controls governing who can access what resources (Security domain).

**ICE Score**: Prioritization framework: Impact × Confidence × Ease (Portfolio domain).

**IDP (Internal Developer Platform)**: Self-service platform enabling product teams to deploy and operate services (Technology domain).

**Impact Mapping**: Strategic planning technique connecting business goals to user behavior to deliverables (Strategy domain).

**Initiative**: Strategic investment area grouping programs and projects aligned to an objective (Strategy domain).

**Integration Architecture**: How systems connect and exchange data (Architecture domain).

**IT Governance**: Processes ensuring IT investments align to business strategy and manage risk (Governance domain).

**JTBD (Jobs-to-be-Done)**: Clayton Christensen framework: customers hire products to do a job (Innovation domain).

## K–L

**KANO Model**: Product prioritization framework: must-be, performance, attractive, indifferent, reverse (Strategy domain).

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**: Measurable metric tracking progress toward an objective (Performance domain).

**Knowledge Graph**: Graph-based knowledge representation showing entities and their relationships (AI domain).

**KYC (Know Your Customer)**: Regulatory process verifying customer identity and assessing risk (Compliance domain).

**Landing Zone**: Pre-configured cloud environment with security, networking, and governance baselines (Technology domain).

**Latency**: Time elapsed between a request and the corresponding response (Technology domain).

**Lean**: Methodology eliminating waste to deliver value faster (Delivery domain).

**LeSS (Large-Scale Scrum)**: Scaled agile framework with single product backlog and multiple teams (Delivery domain).

**LLM (Large Language Model)**: AI model trained on large text corpora; generates, analyzes, and transforms text (AI domain).

**LLM Gateway**: Enterprise component managing all LLM API calls: routing, rate limiting, cost control, observability (AI domain).

**LLMOps**: MLOps practices extended for LLM-specific concerns: prompts, RAG, evaluation, guardrails (AI domain).

**LPM (Lean Portfolio Management)**: SAFe approach connecting strategy to execution through value stream funding (Portfolio domain).

## M

**Market Segment**: Subset of a market with distinct characteristics or needs (Strategy domain).

**McKinsey 7S**: Framework examining strategy, structure, systems, staff, skills, style, shared values (Strategy domain).

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: Anthropic-led protocol for tool connectivity to AI models (AI domain).

**Memory (AI Agent)**: Agent's ability to store and retrieve information: episodic, semantic, procedural (AI domain).

**Microservices**: Architectural style with fine-grained, independently deployable services (Architecture domain).

**Mission**: Statement of what an organization does and for whom (Strategy domain).

**ML (Machine Learning)**: AI technique where systems learn from data without explicit programming (AI domain).

**MLOps**: Practices for deploying, monitoring, and maintaining ML models in production (AI domain).

**Model Drift**: Degradation of model performance over time as real-world data changes (AI domain).

**Model Registry**: Versioned catalog of all ML and AI models including metadata and evaluation results (AI domain).

**Model Risk Management**: Governance framework for identifying, measuring, and managing risks from AI/ML models (Risk domain).

**Multi-Agent System**: Architecture with multiple AI agents collaborating to accomplish complex tasks (AI domain).

## N–O

**Network Organization**: Enterprise functioning as ecosystem orchestrator coordinating external partners (Organization domain).

**NPV (Net Present Value)**: Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment (Finance domain).

**OKR (Objectives and Key Results)**: Goal-setting framework with qualitative objective and quantitative key results (Performance domain).

**OpEx (Operating Expenditure)**: Day-to-day costs of running the business (Finance domain).

**Operating Model**: How an organization delivers value: people, process, technology, data, governance (Operating Model domain).

**Orchestration**: Central coordinator directing all participants in a process or workflow (Architecture domain).

**Organization Design**: Discipline of aligning structure, governance, roles, and culture to strategy (Organization domain).

## P

**P&L (Profit and Loss)**: Financial statement showing revenues, costs, and profitability (Finance domain).

**PI (Program Increment)**: SAFe's 10–12 week planning and delivery cadence (Delivery domain).

**Platform**: Foundation enabling others to create value on top; governed by platform economics (Strategy domain).

**Platform Engineering**: Building and maintaining internal developer platforms (Technology domain).

**Platform Operating Model**: Organization operating as a platform business providing capabilities as services (Operating Model domain).

**PMO (Project/Programme Management Office)**: Function providing project governance, standards, and reporting (Governance domain).

**Portfolio**: Collection of programs and projects aligned to a strategic theme (Portfolio domain).

**Porter's Five Forces**: Industry analysis framework examining competitive dynamics (Strategy domain).

**Precision**: Of all positive AI predictions, percentage that are actually positive (AI domain).

**Prioritization**: Process of ranking initiatives by relative importance and urgency (Portfolio domain).

**Process**: Sequence of activities producing a defined outcome (Architecture domain).

**Program**: Coordinated group of related projects managed to obtain benefits not available individually (Portfolio domain).

**Project**: Temporary endeavor with defined start, end, and deliverable (Portfolio domain).

**Prompt**: Input instruction given to an LLM to elicit a desired output (AI domain).

**Prompt Engineering**: Craft of designing effective prompts to guide LLM behavior without changing model weights (AI domain).

**Prompt Registry**: Version-controlled, governed storage for prompt templates (AI domain).

**Purpose**: Why an organization exists beyond profit; its societal contribution (Strategy domain).

## Q–R

**RACI**: Responsibility assignment matrix: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed (Governance domain).

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Technique injecting retrieved relevant context into LLM prompt at query time (AI domain).

**Recall**: Of all actual positives, percentage correctly identified by AI model (AI domain).

**Reference Architecture**: Prescriptive pattern for a class of architectural problems; reusable across solutions (Architecture domain).

**RICE Score**: Prioritization framework: Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort (Portfolio domain).

**Risk Appetite**: Amount of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its strategy (Risk domain).

**Risk Register**: Record of identified risks, their likelihood, impact, and mitigations (Risk domain).

**Roadmap**: Strategic view of what will be built or changed over time at quarter/half year granularity (Strategy domain).

**ROI (Return on Investment)**: Benefit from an investment as a percentage of its cost (Finance domain).

**Run/Grow/Transform (RGT)**: Gartner IT budget classification: operational (Run), growth (Grow), disruptive (Transform) (Portfolio domain).

## S

**SABSA**: Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture; business-driven security architecture (Architecture domain).

**SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework)**: Framework scaling agile from team to enterprise level (Delivery domain).

**SBB (Solution Building Block)**: TOGAF concept of specific implementation of an ABB (Architecture domain).

**Scrum**: Agile framework using sprints, daily standups, and retrospectives (Delivery domain).

**Service Mesh**: Infrastructure layer managing east-west service-to-service communication (Technology domain).

**SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: Agreed level of service: availability, latency, error rate (Technology domain).

**SLM (Small Language Model)**: Compact language model optimized for efficiency (AI domain).

**Solution Architecture**: Specific design for a specific system or product (Architecture domain).

**Span of Control**: Number of direct reports a manager has (Organization domain).

**Sprint**: Fixed-length iteration (typically 2 weeks) in which a team completes defined scope (Delivery domain).

**Story (User Story)**: Short description of a feature from the user's perspective (Delivery domain).

**Strategic Initiative**: Program of work enabling a strategic objective (Strategy domain).

**Strategic Intent**: Competitive ambition describing where the organization wants to lead (Strategy domain).

**Strategic Theme**: Annual focus area providing direction for investment and delivery (Strategy domain).

**Strategy Map**: Balanced Scorecard tool showing cause-and-effect relationships between objectives (Strategy domain).

**Streaming (Data)**: Continuous, real-time data processing as events occur (Technology domain).

## T

**Target Operating Model (TOM)**: Blueprint of how an organization needs to operate in the future to execute its strategy (Operating Model domain).

**Team Topologies**: Matthew Skelton/Manuel Pais model of four team types and three interaction modes (Organization domain).

**Three Horizons**: McKinsey framework: H1 (core), H2 (emerging), H3 (exploratory) investments (Strategy domain).

**Throughput**: Number of requests processed per unit of time (Technology domain).

**TOGAF**: The Open Group Architecture Framework; enterprise architecture methodology (Architecture domain).

**Token**: Sub-word unit that LLMs process; typically 3/4 of a word; key unit of LLM cost (AI domain).

**TOM**: See Target Operating Model (Operating Model domain).

**Transformation Office**: Dedicated organizational function governing large-scale change programs (Governance domain).

**Two-Pizza Rule**: Amazon principle: no team should require more than two pizzas to feed (5–8 people) (Organization domain).
