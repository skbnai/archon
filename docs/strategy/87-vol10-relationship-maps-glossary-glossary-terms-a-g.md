# Enterprise Glossary: A-G

Why this matters: Precise terminology prevents miscommunication. This glossary defines 250+ enterprise terms used throughout strategy, architecture, and AI contexts.

## A

**A2A (Agent-to-Agent)**: Protocol enabling AI agents to communicate and collaborate across organizational and system boundaries (AI domain).

**ABB (Architecture Building Block)**: TOGAF concept defining a capability without specifying implementation; technology-agnostic (Architecture domain).

**Accountability**: In RACI, the single person who owns the outcome and is answerable for it (Governance domain).

**Agile**: Iterative, collaborative approach to software delivery valuing individuals and interactions over processes and tools (Delivery domain).

**AI Agent**: Autonomous AI system that plans and executes multi-step tasks using tools without step-by-step human instruction (AI domain).

**AI COE**: AI Center of Excellence; organizational construct centralizing AI expertise, standards, and governance (Organization domain).

**AI Ethics**: Principles governing responsible AI development and use: fairness, transparency, accountability, safety (AI domain).

**AI Factory**: Industrial-scale operating model treating AI use case development as a repeatable manufacturing process (Operating Model domain).

**AI Governance**: Policies, processes, and controls ensuring AI systems are developed and used responsibly (Governance domain).

**AI Maturity Model**: Framework assessing organizational readiness for AI development and deployment at scale (Strategy domain).

**AI Operating Model**: How an organization develops, deploys, governs, and continuously improves AI capabilities (Operating Model domain).

**AI Platform**: Shared infrastructure enabling AI delivery: LLM gateway, model registry, evaluation (Technology domain).

**AI Risk**: Potential harm from AI systems: bias, hallucination, security vulnerabilities, unintended consequences (Risk domain).

**Algorithm**: Step-by-step procedure for solving a problem or making a decision (Technology domain).

**AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**: Regulatory requirements and controls to detect and prevent money laundering (Compliance domain).

**Ansoff Matrix**: Strategic framework for growth: market penetration, product development, market development, diversification (Strategy domain).

**API (Application Programming Interface)**: Contract defining how software components communicate (Technology domain).

**API Gateway**: Component managing north-south API traffic: authentication, rate limiting, routing, transformation (Architecture domain).

**Architecture Domain**: TOGAF classification of one of four high-level architecture areas: Business, Data, Application, Technology (Architecture domain).

**Architecture Principle**: Fundamental rule guiding architecture decisions across the enterprise (Architecture domain).

**Architecture Review Board (ARB)**: Governance body reviewing and approving architecture decisions and standards (Governance domain).

**Architecture Runway**: SAFe concept of pre-existing infrastructure enabling near-term feature development (Delivery domain).

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: Machine simulation of cognitive functions including learning, reasoning, and problem-solving (AI domain).

**ART (Agile Release Train)**: SAFe concept of a long-lived team of teams (50–125 people) delivering value in synchronized cadence (Delivery domain).

**Autonomy**: Ability of an AI agent to act without continuous human direction (AI domain).

## B

**Balanced Scorecard**: Performance management framework with four perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Process, Learning (Strategy domain).

**Benefits Realization**: Process of measuring whether expected benefits from an investment are actually achieved (Portfolio domain).

**BCG Matrix**: Boston Consulting Group portfolio tool: Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs (Strategy domain).

**BIZBoK**: Business Architecture Guild body of knowledge covering capabilities, value streams, organization (Architecture domain).

**Blue Ocean Strategy**: Creating uncontested market space rather than competing in existing markets (Strategy domain).

**Board of Directors**: Governing body of an organization with fiduciary responsibility and strategic direction (Governance domain).

**Business Architecture**: Architecture domain covering strategy, capabilities, processes, and organization (Architecture domain).

**Business Capability**: What an organization can do; stable over time regardless of organizational changes (Architecture domain).

**Business Case**: Document justifying an investment by quantifying expected costs, benefits, and risks (Finance domain).

**Business Domain**: Major area of business activity containing related capabilities (Architecture domain).

**Business Model Canvas**: Osterwalder framework with 9 building blocks describing how an organization creates and captures value (Strategy domain).

**Business Process**: Sequence of activities producing an outcome; expresses how a capability is executed (Architecture domain).

**Business Transformation**: Fundamental change to an organization's strategy, operating model, or capabilities (Strategy domain).

## C

**CapEx (Capital Expenditure)**: Investment in long-lived assets expected to provide benefit beyond one year (Finance domain).

**Capability Maturity**: Level at which a capability is performed; typically 1 (initial) to 5 (optimizing) (Architecture domain).

**CAIO (Chief AI Officer)**: Executive responsible for AI strategy, governance, and delivery across the enterprise (Leadership domain).

**CDO (Chief Data Officer)**: Executive responsible for data strategy, governance, and data-driven value creation (Leadership domain).

**Change Management**: Structured approach to transitioning individuals and organizations to a desired future state (People domain).

**CISO (Chief Information Security Officer)**: Executive responsible for information security strategy and risk management (Leadership domain).

**Cloud Operating Model**: Governance model for enterprise use of public cloud services (Operating Model domain).

**CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration)**: Framework measuring process capability maturity on a 1–5 scale (Quality domain).

**Cognitive Load**: Amount of mental effort required to understand and manage a system or process (Organization domain).

**Conway's Law**: Organizations produce system designs that mirror their communication structures (Architecture domain).

**CoP (Community of Practice)**: Group of practitioners sharing knowledge and developing skills in a common domain (Knowledge domain).

**Corporate Strategy**: High-level plan for achieving organizational goals and competitive advantage (Strategy domain).

**CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote)**: Software enabling complex product configuration and pricing (Technology domain).

**Cross-Functional Team**: Team with members from multiple disciplines working toward a shared outcome (Organization domain).

**Customer Journey Map**: Visual representation of the end-to-end customer experience with an organization (Strategy domain).

## D

**DACI**: Decision framework: Driver, Approver, Contributors, Informed (Governance domain).

**Data Architecture**: Architecture domain covering data assets, data flows, and data management (Architecture domain).

**Data Domain**: Group of related data entities owned by a business area (Architecture domain).

**Data Fabric**: Connected metadata layer enabling unified data access across distributed sources (Technology domain).

**Data Governance**: Policies and processes ensuring data quality, security, and appropriate use (Governance domain).

**Data Mesh**: Decentralized data architecture with domain ownership and self-serve data infrastructure (Architecture domain).

**Data Operating Model**: How enterprise data is owned, managed, shared, and consumed to create value (Operating Model domain).

**Data Product**: Data asset treated as a product: discoverable, versioned, SLA-governed, owned (Architecture domain).

**Decision Rights**: Who has authority to make which decisions (Governance domain).

**Deep Learning**: Subset of ML using neural networks with many layers to learn complex patterns (AI domain).

**Design Thinking**: Human-centered innovation approach: empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test (Innovation domain).

**DevOps**: Culture and practice combining software development and operations for faster delivery (Delivery domain).

**Digital Operating Model**: Operating model built for continuous delivery of digital products and services (Operating Model domain).

**Digital Twin**: Virtual representation of a physical object or system, updated in real time (Technology domain).

**Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**: Software design approach aligning code structure with business domain structure (Architecture domain).

## E

**EA (Enterprise Architecture)**: Discipline aligning an organization's processes, information, and technology to its strategy (Architecture domain).

**Embedding**: Dense vector representation of text capturing semantic meaning (AI domain).

**Enterprise Architecture Framework**: Structured methodology for planning and governing enterprise architecture (Architecture domain).

**Enterprise Strategy**: Organization-wide plan for achieving long-term competitive advantage (Strategy domain).

**Epic**: Large body of user work representing a business outcome; contains multiple features (Delivery domain).

**ESB (Enterprise Service Bus)**: Middleware enabling service-oriented architecture integration (Technology domain).

**Evaluation (AI)**: Systematic assessment of AI system quality: accuracy, safety, helpfulness (AI domain).

**Event-Driven Architecture**: Systems communicating through events rather than direct API calls (Architecture domain).

**Executive Sponsor**: Senior leader accountable for a program's business case and outcomes (Governance domain).

**Explainability**: Ability to explain why an AI system produced a specific output (AI domain).

## F

**Feature (Delivery)**: User-visible capability that delivers business value; delivered within a sprint or two (Delivery domain).

**Feature Store**: Central store of ML features ensuring consistency between training and inference (AI domain).

**Federation (Governance)**: Distribution of authority between central function and decentralized business units (Governance domain).

**Fine-tuning**: Training a pre-trained model on domain-specific data to adapt its behavior (AI domain).

**FinOps**: Financial operations for cloud: optimizing cloud spend with cost accountability (Finance domain).

**Five Forces (Porter's)**: Framework analyzing industry competitiveness (Strategy domain).

**Foundation Model**: Large AI model trained on broad data; general-purpose; fine-tunable for specific use cases (AI domain).

**Function (Organization)**: Organizational unit responsible for a domain of work (Finance, HR, IT, Operations) (Organization domain).

## G

**GenAI (Generative AI)**: AI that generates new content: text, images, code, audio, video (AI domain).

**GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)**: Integrated framework for managing enterprise governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance (Governance domain).

**Guardrails (AI)**: Controls preventing AI systems from generating harmful, inaccurate, or non-compliant outputs (AI domain).

**Governance**: System of rules, practices, and processes directing and controlling an organization (Governance domain).
